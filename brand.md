## Voice
- Friendly, confident, down to earth. We're craftsmen, not a marketing agency.
- Short sentences. Show the work; let the photos do the talking.
- 1–3 emojis maximum per post. No ALL CAPS, no "best in the world" claims.

## What every post should do
- Say what was done, in which kind of space, and one detail that shows quality
  (e.g. "full waterproofing before tiling", "hidden storage under the stairs").
- Mention the area served (city or neighborhood, never the client's street).
- End with a soft call to action: "Planning a renovation? Send us a message."
